Safety & Medical Disclaimer

VitalLens is an AI-powered health information tool designed to assist and educate. It is not intended to replace professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

✅ What VitalLens Does:

  • • Provides AI-powered analysis of medical images
  • • Offers health information and educational content
  • • Suggests when to seek professional medical care
  • • Helps you understand medical terminology

⚠️ What VitalLens Cannot Do:

  • • Replace professional medical diagnosis
  • • Provide treatment recommendations
  • • Handle medical emergencies
  • • Guarantee 100% accuracy in all cases

Important Reminders:

  • • Always consult with qualified healthcare professionals for medical decisions

  • • Use VitalLens as a supplementary tool, not a replacement for medical care
  • • Results are for informational purposes only
